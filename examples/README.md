# Examples — before / after

Short, anonymised walk-throughs of the pack in action. Each shows the **diagnosis on the served HTML**, the **fix**, the **verification on the served HTML**, and the **report** — the exact shape every skill follows.

All sites, code, and figures are illustrative and anonymised. Numbers are realistic but not from a specific client.
