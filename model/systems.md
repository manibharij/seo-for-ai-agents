# The five systems

Visibility is produced by five systems, kept by one loop, and judged on many surfaces.

The systems are separated by **your relationship to control**, because that is what
decides whether an agent may act, advise, or only observe.

| System | Relationship | What an agent may do |
|---|---|---|
Demand | Observed | Read it and choose. Never invent it |
Identity | Built | Diagnose and change, under approval |
Substance | Built | Diagnose and change, under approval |
Access | Built | Diagnose and change, under approval |
Distribution | Earned | Analyse and advise. Never execute |

---

## Demand (observed)

What people and machines actually ask, per surface. Query demand, prompt demand,
questions, seasonality, intent mix, which competitors already hold the demand, and
which demand is worth having at all.

You do not control demand. Reading it wrongly is the most expensive mistake available,
because every other system is then pointed at the wrong target.

## Identity (built)

Who you provably are. Not your website: **you**. The name, the places, the people, the
credentials, the proof, the schema entity graph, the consistency of all of it across
every surface where you appear, and the record of your existence in public knowledge
bases.

Identity is the system most often skipped and the one that decides whether an assistant
can name you at all, because an engine that cannot resolve who you are has nothing to
recommend.

## Substance (built)

What you actually offer and say. Intent match, depth, information gain, coverage of the
territory, media, products, and the honesty of every claim.

No amount of Access work rescues a page that has nothing to say. No amount of Substance
work rescues a page a machine cannot reach.

## Access (built)

Whether a machine can get to it, read it, understand it and move through it.

**The Visibility Ladder is this system's internal dependency order**, and within Access
it still holds exactly as before:

1. **Reach.** Can a machine get to the content at all?
2. **Read.** Once reached, is there real content, served, fast and usable?
3. **Understand.** Can the engine identify the entities on the page?
4. **Connect.** Is the page wired into a coherent site?

Diagnose top to bottom, fix bottom to top. The rule is unchanged: **verify on served
output, never on source**.

Access also carries the questions the original ladder had no room for: rendering for AI
crawlers specifically, bot access policy, feeds, and the interfaces built for agents
rather than for crawlers.

> **Rank is no longer a rung.** It was rung five, and it is now the **outcome the whole
> model produces**. Its old contents, intent match and quality and experience evidence
> and topical authority, belong to Substance and Identity, which is where the work
> actually happens. Ranking remains the priority. It was never a step in a ladder; it
> is the result of all five systems being right at once.

## Distribution (earned, advisory)

Whether anything outside your control points at you or stocks you. Links, mentions,
directories, marketplaces, communities, syndication.

**Advisory by design.** The agent analyses the gap, finds the angle and writes the
strategy. It never executes outreach, never arranges placement and never buys anything.
That boundary is not timidity, it is the same honesty that stops it fabricating an
author.

## The Operations loop

Measurement instrumentation, baselines, verification on served output, regression
detection, monitoring, alerting, reporting, and the work queue itself.

Operations is a loop rather than a system because visibility decays. A fix that was
verified in March can be undone by a deploy in April, and a model that treats the work
as finished will quietly lose everything it won. Every finding that was fixed stays
watched, and a regression reopens itself.

---

## How the systems relate

Dependency runs in one direction only where Access is concerned: nothing downstream
matters if a machine cannot reach the page. Everything else is **simultaneous, not
sequential**. A perfect Access score with no Demand reading is a fast, well-formed,
beautifully structured page that nobody was looking for.

That is the substantive correction to the old single-ladder frame. Visibility is not a
climb. It is five systems held true at once, and kept that way.
