# Invariants

Rules that hold in every system, every job, every role and both runtimes. A workflow
that breaks one of these is wrong, however good its output looks.

## 1. Verify on served output, never on source

Source code is an intention. Served output is the fact. Every finding, every fix and
every completion claim is checked against what a machine actually receives: rendered
HTML, the response headers, the SERP, the API payload. A role may not mark work done on
the strength of a diff.

## 2. Only established facts may be published

Every fact in the context pack carries its provenance:

`established` · `inferred` · `unknown` · `conflict`

**Only `established` facts may reach published copy, schema, or a claim made to a
customer.** An inferred fact may guide strategy and must never be asserted. This single
rule is what converts "never fabricate" from a refusal into a method, and it applies to
drafting, to schema, to reports and to anything a role says in a thread.

## 3. Owned media is built, earned media is advised

Identity, Substance and Access are yours: diagnose and change them, under approval.
Distribution is not: analyse it, strategise it, never execute it. No outreach, no
placement, no purchase, in any form.

## 4. Some things are permanently human

These are escalations, never tasks:

- Anything needing a credential, an identity or a relationship: verification,
  ownership, claiming, outreach.
- Any claim that cannot be sourced to an `established` fact: authors, reviews,
  qualifications, results, and anything medical, legal or financial.
- Judgement calls on tone or risk in public replies.

A role that meets one of these stops and says so. It does not improvise.

## 5. Do not regress an existing site

Treat existing canonicals, redirects, noindex directives and hreflang as intentional
until proven otherwise. Preserve URLs. Flag large changes rather than making them. The
cost of breaking a site that already works exceeds the benefit of almost any fix.

## 6. Talk to the agent, serve the human

Workflows are written for an agent to execute, and every output is written for a
non-specialist to read. A finding that only an SEO can understand has failed half its
job.

## 7. No build-time capability is ever paywalled

The method, the workflows, the roles and the contracts are open and run free with your
own keys. The paid platform sells continuity: data, memory, scheduling, supervision and
evidence history. It never sells back a capability the open source could have performed.

## 8. State what is unknown

Absence of evidence is reported as absence, not as zero and never as a guess.
Assistant surfaces in particular cannot be measured the way rankings can, and the
honest position is also the defensible one.
